Workshop Services

All makes and models. From routine maintenance to compliance work — done methodically, the first time.

01 / Servicing
Logbook Servicing

Keep your warranty intact.

When you buy a new car, your manufacturer warranty offers peace of mind — but only when servicing is done correctly. We perform full logbook servicing on all makes and models of passenger and light commercial vehicles, including European prestige and exotics. We catch issues early and keep your warranty fully intact.

  • All makes & models, including exotics and prestige
  • Genuine and OEM-equivalent parts
  • Manufacturer warranty preserved
  • Full service report with every visit
02 / Compliance
Safety Inspections

e-Safety, identity and pre-purchase.

As a workshop authorised by the NSW Department of Roads & Maritime Services, we issue e-Safety checks (formerly pink slips) for vehicles over five years old, vehicle identity checks for lapsed registrations, and thorough pre-purchase inspections.

  • RMS-authorised e-Safety checks
  • Vehicle identity inspections
  • Pre-purchase reports
  • Detailed list of any required repairs
03 / Drivetrain
Brake & Clutch

Stopping power, restored.

Stop-start city driving wears brakes and clutches faster than most drivers expect. We thoroughly inspect both systems and fit replacement pads, discs, drums and clutch hydraulics as required. Brake discs and flywheels are machined on-site, saving you the wait for sub-contracted work.

  • Brake pads, discs and drums
  • Clutch hydraulics and replacement
  • On-site disc & flywheel machining
  • Performance and OEM options available
04 / Handling
Suspension & Steering

Compliance, comfort, control.

Body roll, vague steering, uneven tyre wear — these symptoms usually trace back to suspension or steering. We replace springs, shocks and bushes, service steering systems, and complete precision wheel balancing and alignment to maximise contact with the road.

  • Springs, shocks and bushes
  • Steering rack and component service
  • 4-wheel alignment
  • Tyre balancing
05 / Exhaust
Exhaust Systems

Repairs and bespoke upgrades.

A faulty exhaust costs you performance, fuel economy, and in worst cases, lets fumes seep into the cabin. We repair existing systems, fit replacement mufflers, and recommend aftermarket headers and extractors for drivers chasing more power and a better note.

  • Exhaust repairs and welding
  • Replacement mufflers & cats
  • Aftermarket headers & extractors
  • Custom system fabrication
